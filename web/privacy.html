PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Wildcardz ("the app") is made by Elvis Antwi Boateng Osei ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

THE SHORT VERSION

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
  • Player names you type stay on your phone.
  • If you use online rooms, your stage name and game answers are sent to the other players in the room while you play, and are not stored.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play / the App Store and our payments provider, RevenueCat.

INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Player names, your settings (sound, age choice, 18+ toggle), your custom deck and game counts are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. We never receive them.

ONLINE ROOMS

When you host or join a room, the app connects to Supabase Realtime (our hosting provider) and shares with the other players in that room: the stage name you enter, a random player ID, and the answers, fake answers and votes you submit. This data passes through Supabase to deliver the game and is not saved in a database by us. When the room closes, it's gone.

PURCHASES

If you buy the VIP pass, the payment is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We use RevenueCat to confirm your purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app, your device's platform and country/currency. We do not receive your payment card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

We do not collect your real name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera data. The app reads your phone's motion sensor only during Charades to detect tilts; this data never leaves the device.

CHILDREN

The app's general content is suitable for teenagers and adults. "Spicy" and "Couples" decks are restricted to users who confirm they are 18 or older. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

DELETING YOUR DATA

Wildcardz has no accounts, so we hold no profile to delete. Everything the app saves (player names, settings, custom decks) lives on your phone: clear the app's storage or uninstall it to delete it. Online room data is never stored. To ask RevenueCat to delete your anonymous purchase record, email us at xelpap@gmail.com and we'll handle the request within 30 days.

YOUR CHOICES

You can delete all locally stored data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Google Play or App Store account settings.

CHANGES

If we change this policy we will update the date above and the in-app link.

CONTACT

Questions: xelpap@gmail.com
