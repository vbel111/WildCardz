COMMON QUESTIONS

  • How do I restore my VIP pass? Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google Play account.
  • How do I cancel a subscription? Open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel Wildcardz. You keep VIP until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Why can't I see the Spicy or Couples decks? They are for players 18 and older. Check your age setting and the "Show 18+ rounds" switch in Settings.
  • Charades tilting feels backwards. Turn on "Flip charades tilt" in Settings.
  • Can't join an online room? Check the 4-letter code and that both phones are online. The host's phone runs the room, so keep it open.
